Documents for GST registration

  • PAN and Aadhaar of proprietor / partners / directors
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Business address proof (rent agreement or electricity bill)
  • Bank account proof (cancelled cheque or statement)
  • Constitution proof (partnership deed / incorporation certificate)
  • Mobile number and email for OTP verification

GST Basics

Understand GST in two minutes

The essentials every business owner should know.

CGST

Central GST

Collected by the Centre on intra-state supplies of goods and services.

SGST

State GST

Collected by the state on intra-state supplies. Split equally with CGST.

IGST

Integrated GST

Charged on inter-state supplies, imports and exports, and shared between Centre and state.

UTGST

Union Territory GST

Applies in place of SGST in Union Territories without a legislature.

GST is mainly charged in 5% and 18% slabs, with a higher rate on select items. The rate depends on the goods or service and can change by notification, so always verify the current rate for your product.

Types of GST registration

  • Regular: for most businesses above the turnover threshold
  • Composition: a simple scheme for small taxpayers with lower compliance
  • Casual taxable person: for temporary stalls and seasonal events
  • Non-resident taxable person: for foreign businesses supplying in India
  • Voluntary: for businesses below the threshold that want to register

Common GST mistakes to avoid

  • Claiming ITC without matching it in GSTR-2B
  • Mismatch between GSTR-1 sales and GSTR-3B liability
  • Wrong HSN/SAC code or tax rate on invoices
  • Filing late and paying avoidable fees and interest
  • Ignoring notices or replying after the deadline
  • Not reconciling books with returns before the annual return

FAQ

Common questions

Who needs GST registration?

Businesses crossing the prescribed turnover limit, or making certain taxable supplies, must register. We can check your case.

How often must returns be filed?

It depends on your registration type and scheme — monthly or quarterly. We set up a calendar for you.

What if I missed a due date?

Late filing can attract fees and interest. Contact us and we will help you regularise it.

What is Input Tax Credit (ITC)?

ITC lets you offset the GST paid on purchases against the GST you collect on sales, which avoids tax on tax. Proper invoices and matching in GSTR-2B are important to claim it.

Can I cancel my GST registration?

Yes, if you close your business, transfer it, or no longer need registration. Pending returns must be filed before cancellation is processed.

I received a GST notice. What should I do?

Don't ignore it. Note the reply deadline, collect the related invoices and returns, and share the notice with us so we can prepare a proper response.

What is the Composition Scheme?

A simplified option for eligible small taxpayers: you pay tax at a fixed percentage of turnover and file fewer returns. You cannot claim input tax credit under it.

Is e-invoicing mandatory for me?

It applies to businesses above a prescribed turnover limit, which the government can revise. Share your turnover with us and we will confirm if it applies.

How long does registration take?

Once documents are ready and verified, the application is usually filed quickly. Approval time depends on the GST department.
